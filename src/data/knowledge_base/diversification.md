
Title: Diversification

AsOf: 2026-02-15

## Summary
Diversification means spreading investments across different assets to reduce the impact of any single investment performing poorly. A diversified portfolio can reduce volatility, but it does not eliminate risk or guarantee profits.

## Key points
- **Risk reduction (not elimination):** Diversification helps reduce the impact of one investment performing badly.
- **Different asset types:** Investors may diversify across stocks, bonds, real estate, and cash equivalents.
- **Within-asset diversification:** Even within stocks, diversification can mean holding companies from different industries, sizes, and regions.
- **Correlation matters:** Assets that move differently from one another can help smooth overall portfolio volatility.

## Common misconceptions
- **“Diversification guarantees profits.”** No. It reduces certain risks but does not eliminate market risk.
- **“Owning many stocks automatically means diversification.”** Not necessarily. If all stocks are in the same sector, risk may still be concentrated.

## Example
Instead of investing all your money in one technology company, you could spread it across a broad market ETF that holds companies from many industries. If one company performs poorly, others may help offset the loss.
